Documents

A general checklist to start with

This is a general list, not a complete official checklist. We adapt it to your visa type and reconfirm the official guide. Items marked [to confirm] will be checked with you before filing.

  • A valid passport, with enough blank pages and typically at least six months’ validity remaining [to confirm for your category]
  • The official application form, completed accurately
  • Photographs that meet the official specification
  • Proof of funds or bank statements covering the period the official guide asks for
  • Travel plan, itinerary or invitation, as relevant to the visa type
  • Evidence of ties to India (employment, studies, family or property), where the official guide asks for it
  • Previous visas or travel history, if any
  • Any supporting letters (employer, university, host) on letterhead
  • A daily itinerary or invitation, in the form the official guide or the local mission asks for

Processing times vary and change. We reconfirm the current official estimate before every filing. Please check the official government or embassy source for the latest figure.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Which Japanese mission will file my visa?

Usually the embassy or consulate that covers your place of residence in India. We confirm the official jurisdiction with you.

Do you guarantee a visa?

No. No honest consultancy can. We prepare a complete, well-checked file and give you straight advice. The decision always belongs to the government, embassy or consulate.

Are you a government or embassy office?

Incredible Travellers is a private visa consultancy and is not affiliated with any government, embassy or consulate. We do not issue visas or make immigration decisions.

Do you handle work visas?

No. We do not offer work-visa cases. We help with tourist and visitor, business, student and family or dependent visas, plus Canada and Australia PR pathways and related services such as Super Visa and spouse or dependent filings.

How long does processing take?

Processing times vary and change. We reconfirm the official estimate before every filing. Always check the official government or embassy source for the current figure.

What does a consultation cost?

You can request a free call-back during office hours (Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM–6:00 PM IST). A paid detailed consultation costs ₹1,000 for 15 minutes and can be booked online.
